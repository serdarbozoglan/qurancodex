It does **not** cover the written content the application serves: the study

`next/public/tefekkur/`, and any other prose content rendered by the site.

## Why content is licensed separately

Much of that content is compiled and paraphrased from copyrighted classical
and contemporary works — academic articles, translated classical tafsīr, and
modern books — each cited by author, title, and page wherever it appears on
the site. That paraphrase is written to stand as this project's own text, but
because it closely follows the structure, selection, and interpretive
choices of specific copyrighted sources, the project is not in a position to
grant a permissive open license (MIT, Creative Commons, or otherwise) over
it — doing so could be read as licensing rights the project does not
independently hold.
